Best Freezer Meals for New Moms

Easy make-ahead meals that help lighten the load after baby arrives.

6 Best Freezer Meals for New Moms

Baked Ziti

Why it works: Comfort food that reheats beautifully.

  • 12 oz pasta
  • 1 lb ground beef or turkey
  • 1 jar marinara
  • 1 cup ricotta
  • 2 cups mozzarella

Instructions

Cook pasta. Brown meat and mix with sauce. Combine pasta with sauce, layer with cheeses, place in baking dish and freeze tightly covered.

Chicken Fajita Freezer Kit

Why it works: Toss into a skillet straight from thawing.

  • 1.5 lbs chicken
  • 3 peppers
  • 1 onion
  • fajita seasoning

Place everything in a freezer bag. Freeze flat. Thaw overnight and cook in a skillet.

Chicken Rice Bowls

  • Cooked chicken
  • Rice
  • Broccoli

Portion rice, chicken, and broccoli into freezer containers. Freeze and reheat when needed.

Hearty Lentil Soup

  • Lentils
  • Carrots
  • Celery
  • Onion
  • Broth

Simmer ingredients until lentils are tender. Cool and freeze in containers.

Breakfast Burritos

  • Eggs
  • Potatoes
  • Cheese
  • Tortillas

Scramble eggs, add filling, roll in tortillas, wrap individually and freeze.

Stuffed Shells

  • Jumbo shells
  • Ricotta
  • Mozzarella
  • Marinara

Fill cooked shells with cheese mixture, cover with sauce and freeze before baking.
