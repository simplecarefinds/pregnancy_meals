Spinach & Feta Omelette

Ingredients
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup spinach
  • 2 tbsp feta
  • Olive oil
Instructions
  • Whisk eggs until fluffy.
  • Sauté spinach lightly.
  • Add eggs and cook slowly.
  • Fold in feta and finish.
Boost iron absorption by pairing with citrus fruits.
