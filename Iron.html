Iron Rich Pregnancy Meals

Spinach & Feta Omelette

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup fresh spinach
  • 2 tbsp feta cheese
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt & pepper

Instructions

  • Whisk eggs with salt and pepper until fluffy.
  • Sauté spinach in olive oil until wilted.
  • Pour eggs over spinach and cook on low heat.
  • Add feta, fold gently, and cook until set.
Chef’s Tip: Pair with vitamin C-rich fruit like oranges to boost iron absorption.

Grilled Steak Power Bowl

Ingredients

  • 150g lean steak
  • 1 cup chickpeas
  • Mixed greens (spinach, kale)
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Olive oil & lemon dressing

Instructions

  • Season steak and grill to desired doneness.
  • Slice thinly and set aside.
  • Assemble greens, chickpeas, and tomatoes in a bowl.
  • Top with steak and drizzle dressing.
Chef’s Tip: Red meat provides heme iron, the most easily absorbed type.

Iron-Rich Steak Dinner

Ingredients

  • 200g beef steak
  • 2 cups sautéed spinach
  • Mashed potatoes
  • Garlic & butter

Instructions

  • Season steak with salt, pepper, and garlic.
  • Sear in butter until golden and cooked to preference.
  • Sauté spinach quickly to retain nutrients.
  • Serve alongside creamy mashed potatoes.
Chef’s Tip: Avoid overcooking spinach to preserve iron content.

Iron Boost Snack Plate

Ingredients

  • Mixed nuts (almonds, cashews)
  • Boiled egg
  • Celery sticks
  • Hummus
  • Dried apricots

Instructions

  • Arrange all ingredients on a serving plate.
  • Slice boiled egg in half and season lightly.
  • Serve with hummus for dipping.
Chef’s Tip: Dried fruits are small but powerful iron boosters—great for quick snacks.
