Breakfast Ideas

Spinach & Feta Omelette

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup fresh spinach
  • 2 tablespoons feta cheese
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: whole grain toast or avocado

Instructions

  1. Warm olive oil in a nonstick skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add spinach and cook for 1 to 2 minutes until wilted.
  3. Whisk eggs with salt and pepper, then pour over the spinach.
  4. Cook gently until the eggs begin to set.
  5. Sprinkle feta on top, fold the omelette, and cook for another minute.
  6. Serve warm with toast or avocado.
Chef Tip: Pair with vitamin C-rich fruit like orange slices or strawberries to support iron absorption.
Lunch Bowls

Grilled Steak Power Bowl

Ingredients

  • 4 ounces grilled steak, sliced
  • 1 cup cooked quinoa or brown rice
  • 1 cup spinach or mixed greens
  • 1/2 cup roasted sweet potatoes
  • 1/4 avocado
  • 1 tablespoon lemon olive oil dressing

Instructions

  1. Cook quinoa or brown rice according to package directions.
  2. Grill steak to a safe internal temperature and let it rest before slicing.
  3. Layer greens, grains, sweet potatoes, avocado, and steak in a bowl.
  4. Drizzle with lemon olive oil dressing.
  5. Serve immediately or pack for lunch.
Nutrition Note: Steak provides heme iron, which is easier for the body to absorb than plant-based iron.
Healthy Dinners

Steak with Spinach & Mashed Potatoes

Ingredients

  • 5 ounces steak
  • 2 cups fresh spinach
  • 1 cup mashed potatoes
  • 1 teaspoon butter or olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • Salt, pepper, and lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Season steak with salt and pepper.
  2. Grill or pan-sear steak until cooked to a safe pregnancy-friendly temperature.
  3. Let steak rest for 5 minutes before slicing.
  4. Sauté spinach with olive oil, garlic, and a squeeze of lemon.
  5. Warm mashed potatoes and plate with steak and spinach.
  6. Serve with extra lemon for brightness.
Chef Tip: Keep the plate simple and balanced: protein, greens, and a comforting carb.
Iron Snacks

Iron Snack Plate

Ingredients

  • 1 hard-boiled egg
  • 1/4 cup hummus
  • Carrot sticks and cucumber slices
  • Small handful of almonds or cashews
  • 2 tablespoons dried apricots or raisins
  • Optional: whole grain crackers

Instructions

  1. Slice the hard-boiled egg in half.
  2. Spoon hummus into a small bowl.
  3. Arrange vegetables, nuts, dried fruit, crackers, and egg on a plate.
  4. Add a pinch of salt, pepper, or paprika to the egg if desired.
  5. Serve as a balanced snack plate between meals.
Nutrition Note: This snack plate combines iron, protein, healthy fats, and fiber for steady energy.
