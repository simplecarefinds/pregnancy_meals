Breakfast Ideas

Spinach & Feta Omelette

Prep: 5 minutes • Cook: 8 minutes • Serves: 1

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup fresh baby spinach, roughly chopped
  • 2 tablespoons pasteurized feta cheese, crumbled
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil or butter
  • 1 tablespoon milk or water for fluffier eggs
  • Pinch of salt and black pepper
  • Optional: sliced avocado, whole grain toast, or orange slices

Instructions

  1. Whisk the eggs with milk or water, salt, and pepper until smooth.
  2. Warm olive oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat.
  3. Add spinach and cook for 1 to 2 minutes until just wilted.
  4. Pour the eggs over the spinach and gently tilt the pan so the eggs spread evenly.
  5. Cook slowly until the edges set, then sprinkle feta over one side.
  6. Fold the omelette in half and cook for another 30 to 60 seconds.
  7. Serve warm with toast and vitamin C-rich fruit to help support iron absorption.
Chef Tip: Keep the heat low. A soft omelette tastes more luxurious and keeps the feta creamy instead of dry.

Grilled Steak Power Bowl

Prep: 15 minutes • Cook: 12 minutes • Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces steak, cooked to pregnancy-safe doneness and sliced
  • 2 cups cooked quinoa, brown rice, or farro
  • 2 cups baby spinach or mixed greens
  • 1 cup roasted sweet potatoes
  • 1/2 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons pumpkin seeds
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • Salt, pepper, and garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Season steak with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  2. Grill or pan-sear until fully cooked to a pregnancy-safe temperature.
  3. Let the steak rest for 5 minutes, then slice thinly across the grain.
  4. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, salt, and pepper to make the dressing.
  5. Add grains to each bowl, then layer spinach, sweet potatoes, tomatoes, avocado, and steak.
  6. Sprinkle with pumpkin seeds and drizzle with dressing.
  7. Serve warm or meal prep in containers with dressing on the side.
Nutrition Note: Steak provides heme iron, while pumpkin seeds, spinach, and grains add plant-based iron and minerals.
Healthy Dinners

Steak with Spinach & Mashed Potatoes

Prep: 15 minutes • Cook: 25 minutes • Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • 10 ounces steak, divided into two portions
  • 3 cups fresh spinach
  • 2 medium potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 2 tablespoons milk
  • 1 tablespoon butter or olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Optional: parsley or chives for garnish

Instructions

  1. Boil potatoes in salted water until fork-tender, about 12 to 15 minutes.
  2. Drain potatoes, then mash with milk, butter, salt, and pepper until creamy.
  3. Season steak with salt and pepper.
  4. Cook steak in a hot skillet until browned and fully cooked to pregnancy-safe doneness.
  5. Rest steak for 5 minutes before slicing.
  6. In the same skillet, add garlic and spinach. Cook until wilted, about 2 minutes.
  7. Finish spinach with lemon juice for brightness.
  8. Plate mashed potatoes, spinach, and sliced steak. Garnish if desired.
Chef Tip: Resting the steak keeps it juicy. The lemon on spinach adds brightness and helps the whole plate feel lighter.
Iron Snacks

Iron Snack Plate

Prep: 10 minutes • Cook: 0 minutes • Serves: 1

Ingredients

  • 1 hard-boiled egg
  • 1/4 cup hummus
  • 1/4 cup almonds, cashews, or pistachios
  • 2 tablespoons dried apricots or raisins
  • Carrot sticks
  • Cucumber slices
  • Bell pepper strips
  • Whole grain crackers or pita wedges
  • Optional: sprinkle of paprika or everything seasoning

Instructions

  1. Slice the hard-boiled egg in half and season lightly.
  2. Spoon hummus into a small bowl and place it on a plate or board.
  3. Arrange carrots, cucumbers, bell peppers, and crackers around the hummus.
  4. Add nuts and dried fruit in small clusters for a styled snack-board look.
  5. Serve immediately or pack in a divided container for an easy pregnancy snack.
Nutrition Note: This snack plate combines protein, fiber, plant-based iron, healthy fats, and slow-digesting carbs.
