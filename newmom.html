Breakfast

Avocado Egg Toast with Tomatoes

A fast breakfast with enough freshness, creaminess, and color to feel like a café plate at home.

10 Minutes High Protein Easy Morning Meal

Ingredients

  • 2 slices sourdough or whole grain bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • Pinch of sea salt
  • Freshly cracked black pepper
  • Pinch of red pepper flakes
  • Optional: fresh parsley or microgreens

Instructions

  1. Toast the bread until golden and crisp.
  2. While the bread toasts, fry or soft-boil the eggs to your liking.
  3. Mash the avocado with a pinch of salt and spread it generously over the toast.
  4. Top with the eggs and add the tomatoes on top or alongside.
  5. Finish with olive oil, black pepper, red pepper flakes, and herbs if using.
Chef tip / nutrition note: Add lemon to the avocado for brightness and freshness. A side of fruit instantly makes this feel more complete and more food-blog worthy on the plate.

Lunch

Lemon Chicken Grain Bowl

A vibrant lunch built from easy shortcuts like cooked grains and rotisserie chicken, styled like a fresh modern café bowl.

15 Minutes Meal Prep Friendly Fresh & Filling

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked quinoa or microwaveable brown rice
  • 1 cup shredded rotisserie chicken
  • 1/2 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cup cucumber, chopped
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 handful baby spinach or arugula
  • 2 tablespoons crumbled feta
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Warm the quinoa or rice if you want the bowl slightly cozy, or leave it chilled for a fresher feel.
  2. Add the grains to a bowl and layer with chicken, avocado, cucumber, tomatoes, greens, and feta.
  3. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper together.
  4. Drizzle over the bowl right before serving.
Chef tip / nutrition note: Keep the toppings grouped instead of tossed if you want a more premium blog-style presentation. It makes the bowl look fuller, brighter, and more intentional.

15-Minute Garlic Salmon with Green Beans

A fast, comforting dinner with enough texture and color to feel elevated, yet easy enough for a weeknight.

15 Minutes One Pan Balanced Dinner

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets
  • 2 cups trimmed green beans
  • 1 cup cooked baby potatoes or microwaveable potatoes
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Optional: chopped parsley and lemon wedges

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Season the salmon with salt and pepper, then cook skin-side down for 4 to 5 minutes.
  3. Turn the salmon and add the green beans and potatoes to the pan.
  4. Add butter and garlic, then spoon the melted garlic butter over the salmon and vegetables.
  5. Cook another 3 to 4 minutes until the salmon is cooked through and the green beans are tender-crisp.
  6. Finish with lemon juice and parsley before serving.
Chef tip / nutrition note: Leave the salmon undisturbed at first so it develops better color. For a more luxurious presentation, plate the salmon first and arrange the vegetables around it.

Peanut Butter Date Energy Bites

Soft, snackable bites with just enough sweetness and texture to feel homemade, polished, and practical.

No Bake Make Ahead One-Handed Snack

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 3/4 cup soft pitted dates
  • 1/3 cup peanut butter or almond butter
  • 2 tablespoons ground flaxseed
  • 2 tablespoons mini chocolate chips
  • 1 tablespoon pumpkin seeds or chopped nuts
  • Pinch of cinnamon
  • Pinch of sea salt

Instructions

  1. Add the dates, peanut butter, oats, flaxseed, cinnamon, and salt to a food processor.
  2. Pulse until the mixture becomes sticky and starts to clump together.
  3. Fold in the chocolate chips and pumpkin seeds.
  4. Roll into small bite-size balls.
  5. Chill for 10 to 15 minutes if you want a firmer texture, then refrigerate.
Chef tip / nutrition note: Roll the bites a little smaller than you think. Smaller bites look prettier on the plate, feel more premium in photos, and are easier to snack on throughout the day.
