12 High Protein Pregnancy Breakfasts

Nourishing breakfasts designed to keep you full, energized, and support a healthy pregnancy.

1. Greek Yogurt Protein Bowl

Creamy Greek yogurt paired with berries and seeds creates a balanced high-protein breakfast that is quick and refreshing.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • ½ cup mixed berries
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tbsp almond butter
  • 1 tsp honey

Instructions

  1. Add yogurt to a bowl.
  2. Top with berries and chia seeds.
  3. Drizzle almond butter and honey.
  4. Serve immediately.

Protein: ~20g

2. Cottage Cheese & Berry Power Bowl

Cottage cheese is packed with protein and calcium — perfect for pregnancy nutrition.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • ½ cup strawberries
  • ¼ cup blueberries
  • 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds
  • 1 tsp honey

Instructions

  1. Spoon cottage cheese into bowl.
  2. Add berries and seeds.
  3. Drizzle honey.

Protein: ~23g

3. Avocado Egg Toast

This savory breakfast combines healthy fats and protein to keep you satisfied all morning.

Ingredients

  • 1 slice whole grain bread
  • ½ avocado
  • 1 egg
  • Salt & pepper

Instructions

  1. Toast bread.
  2. Mash avocado and spread.
  3. Top with fried egg.
  4. Season and serve.

Protein: ~15g

4. Protein Smoothie

A quick breakfast for busy mornings — smooth, filling, and packed with nutrients.

Ingredients

  • 1 banana
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • ½ cup Greek yogurt
  • ½ cup frozen berries

Instructions

  1. Add all ingredients to blender.
  2. Blend until smooth.
  3. Serve immediately.

Protein: ~22g

5. Egg & Spinach Breakfast Muffins

Perfect for meal prep — bake once and enjoy all week.

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • ½ cup spinach
  • ¼ cup shredded cheese
  • Salt & pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Whisk eggs.
  3. Add spinach and cheese.
  4. Pour into muffin tin.
  5. Bake 18 minutes.

Protein: ~18g

6. Peanut Butter Banana Oatmeal

A warm comforting breakfast rich in protein and fiber.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup oats
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • ½ banana

Instructions

  1. Cook oats with milk.
  2. Add banana and peanut butter.
  3. Stir and serve.

Protein: ~16g

7. Chia Seed Protein Pudding

Make overnight for a simple nutritious breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tsp honey
  • ½ cup berries

Instructions

  1. Mix chia seeds and milk.
  2. Refrigerate overnight.
  3. Top with berries and honey.

Protein: ~14g

8. Breakfast Burrito

A hearty protein packed breakfast with eggs and beans.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • ¼ cup black beans
  • Whole wheat tortilla
  • ¼ avocado

Instructions

  1. Scramble eggs.
  2. Add beans.
  3. Fill tortilla.
  4. Top with avocado.

Protein: ~20g

9. Protein Pancakes

Fluffy pancakes with extra protein for sustained energy.

Ingredients

  • 1 banana
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup oats

Instructions

  1. Blend ingredients.
  2. Cook like pancakes.
  3. Serve with berries.

Protein: ~18g

10. Smoked Salmon Toast

A nutrient dense breakfast rich in protein and omega-3s.

Ingredients

  • Whole grain toast
  • 2 oz smoked salmon
  • Cream cheese
  • Dill

Instructions

  1. Toast bread.
  2. Spread cream cheese.
  3. Add salmon.
  4. Top with dill.

Protein: ~21g

11. Quinoa Breakfast Bowl

Quinoa is a complete protein and perfect for pregnancy nutrition.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup cooked quinoa
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1 tbsp almonds
  • ½ banana

Instructions

  1. Warm quinoa with milk.
  2. Add banana and almonds.
  3. Serve warm.

Protein: ~17g

12. Almond Butter Apple Toast

A simple high protein breakfast that takes less than 5 minutes.

Ingredients

  • Whole grain toast
  • 1 tbsp almond butter
  • Apple slices
  • Cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Toast bread.
  2. Spread almond butter.
  3. Top with apples and cinnamon.

Protein: ~14g
