🥑 Classic Favorite

Smashed Avocado & Poached Egg Toast

Creamy avocado piled on hearty sourdough with a perfectly poached egg. Rich in folate, healthy fats, and protein — a true pregnancy powerhouse.

Folate Protein 18g Healthy Fats Iron

Ingredients (Serves 1)

  • 2 slices sourdough bread, toasted
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Red pepper flakes, salt & pepper
  • Optional: cherry tomatoes, everything bagel seasoning

Instructions

  1. Toast sourdough to golden. Halve, pit, and scoop avocado into a bowl. Add lemon juice, salt, and pepper; smash with a fork to your preferred texture.
  2. Bring a small saucepan of water to a gentle simmer. Add a splash of white vinegar. Crack each egg into a small cup, swirl the water, and slide eggs in. Poach 3–4 minutes until whites are completely set.
  3. Spread avocado on toast, top with poached eggs, a pinch of red pepper flakes, and everything bagel seasoning if using. Serve immediately.

🫐 No-Cook Wonder

Triple Berry Greek Yogurt Parfait

Layers of thick Greek yogurt, antioxidant-rich berries, and crunchy granola. Sky-high in calcium and probiotics to support digestion during pregnancy.

Calcium Probiotics Antioxidants Protein 20g

Ingredients (Serves 1)

  • 1 cup full-fat Greek yogurt
  • ½ cup mixed berries (blueberries, raspberries, strawberries)
  • ¼ cup granola (low-sugar preferred)
  • 1 tbsp honey or pure maple syrup
  • 1 tsp chia seeds

Instructions

  1. Spoon half the Greek yogurt into a tall glass or jar.
  2. Add half the berries and a sprinkle of granola. Repeat layers with remaining yogurt and berries.
  3. Top with remaining granola, a drizzle of honey, and chia seeds. Serve immediately or refrigerate overnight for a grab-and-go breakfast.
🥣 Most-Saved Recipe

Banana Walnut Oatmeal with Iron Boost

A warm, comforting bowl that checks every pregnancy nutrition box. Oats are loaded with fiber and iron, walnuts deliver omega-3s for baby's brain development, and banana adds natural sweetness plus potassium to fight leg cramps.

Iron Omega-3s Fiber Potassium Magnesium

Ingredients (Serves 1)

  • ½ cup old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 1 cup whole milk or oat milk
  • 1 ripe banana, sliced
  • 2 tbsp chopped walnuts
  • 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
  • Pinch of sea salt

Instructions

  1. Combine oats, milk, cinnamon, and salt in a small saucepan over medium heat. Stir frequently and cook 5–7 minutes until thick and creamy.
  2. Mash half the banana into the oats and stir — this naturally sweetens without extra sugar.
  3. Pour into a bowl. Top with remaining banana slices, walnuts, flaxseed, and a drizzle of maple syrup. Serve hot.
  4. Meal prep tip: Cook a big batch, refrigerate up to 4 days, reheat with a splash of milk.

🥚 Meal Prep Hero

Spinach, Feta & Sun-Dried Tomato Egg Muffins

Bake once, eat all week. These portable egg bites are loaded with folate-rich spinach and calcium from feta. Grab two on the way out the door — zero excuses needed.

Folate Calcium Protein 14g Iron

Ingredients (Makes 12)

  • 8 large eggs
  • ¼ cup whole milk
  • 1 cup fresh baby spinach, roughly chopped
  • ⅓ cup crumbled feta cheese
  • ¼ cup sun-dried tomatoes, drained & chopped
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder
  • Cooking spray

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Spray a 12-cup muffin tin generously with cooking spray.
  2. Whisk eggs, milk, salt, pepper, and garlic powder in a large bowl until fully combined.
  3. Divide spinach, feta, and sun-dried tomatoes evenly among muffin cups. Pour egg mixture over each, filling ¾ full.
  4. Bake 18–22 minutes until puffed and set. Cool 5 minutes before removing. Refrigerate up to 5 days; reheat 30 seconds in the microwave.

🥤 Morning Sickness Friendly

Green Goddess Smoothie Bowl

Cold, creamy, and easy on a queasy stomach. Frozen mango and banana mask the spinach completely. Loaded with folate, vitamin C, and iron — especially great in the first trimester.

Folate Vitamin C Iron DHA

Ingredients (Serves 1)

  • 1 cup frozen mango chunks
  • 1 frozen ripe banana
  • 2 cups fresh baby spinach
  • ½ cup coconut milk or almond milk
  • 1 tsp spirulina powder (optional)
  • Toppings: sliced kiwi, granola, hemp seeds, coconut flakes

Instructions

  1. Add coconut milk to blender first, then spinach. Blend until silky smooth — no green flecks.
  2. Add frozen mango and banana. Blend on high until thick and creamy. It should be spoonable; add milk 1 tbsp at a time only if needed.
  3. Pour into a wide bowl. Arrange toppings: sliced kiwi, granola, hemp seeds, and coconut flakes. Eat immediately.

👩‍⚕️ Chef's Pregnancy Nutrition Tips

🍊

Pair iron with vitamin CEat iron-rich foods (oats, spinach, eggs) alongside vitamin C sources like berries or citrus to boost absorption by up to 3×.

❄️

Batch freeze everythingMake extra smoothie packs, egg muffins, and overnight oats on Sunday. Future tired-you will be very grateful.

🧂

Watch sodium in fetaRinse crumbled feta under cold water to cut salt content by up to 30% without losing that tangy flavor.

🥚

Always cook eggs fullyDuring pregnancy, ensure all eggs are cooked through — whites must be completely set, no runny whites at all.
