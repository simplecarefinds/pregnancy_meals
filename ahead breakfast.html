Postpartum Snacks

Simple to Prep & Easy to Eat!

When your hands are full and your energy is low, the best snacks are the ones you can grab, hold, and finish quickly. These four ideas are soft, satisfying, and easy to keep ready for those long newborn days.

Fruit & Nut Cups

Portion trail mix, roasted nuts, and dried fruit into small cups so they are ready to grab with one hand.

Protein Bars

Choose bars with oats, nut butter, and seeds for something filling that can live in your bag or nightstand.
